## Taking part in an EcoShift test session

**Who we are.** We are Shaurya, Arnav and Ghrini, three high school students who built a free website called EcoShift for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge. EcoShift uses past weather to estimate how different crop rotations might have done on a field.

**Why we are asking.** We want to find out whether the tool helps farmers and advisers make better-informed choices, and what is confusing or wrong in it. We are not testing you.

**What you will do.** A session lasts about 45 minutes. You will try six short tasks on the website while saying aloud what you are thinking, answer a few questions, and fill in a short survey.

**What we will record.** A student will take notes. With your permission, we will also record your screen and voice so we can check our notes. The recording is only used by the three students and the supervising teacher to write our report. We will not post recordings online.

**Risks and benefits.** We do not expect any risk beyond the time it takes. There is no payment. You may help other farmers and students by telling us what does not work. The tool gives estimates, not advice. Please do not make real planting or financial decisions based only on it.

**Your choices.** Taking part is voluntary. You can skip any question, stop at any time, and ask us to delete your notes and recording afterwards. You can ask this by contacting the teacher listed below. You do not need to give a reason.

**Privacy.** We will label your notes with a code (such as P3), not your name. We will keep the list that links names to codes private and delete it after the project ends. We will not collect your address or the location of your fields unless you choose to import a field, and we will not publish field outlines.
